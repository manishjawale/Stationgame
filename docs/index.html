How to play
  • Tap a passenger — they walk in the direction of their arrow.
  • Orange, with a bag = getting off. They step out of the door and walk to the stairs.
  • Everyone else is boarding. Shirt colour = their train. When they reach a door of their train, they turn and get on.
  • If someone getting off is still in the doorway, nobody can board — let them get off first! Pillars and the chai stall never move.
  • Finish faster for more stars and coins.
