## Full description
All aboard the busiest platform in Mumbai! 🚆

Next Station is a relaxing-but-tricky tap puzzle set on a Mumbai local train platform. Tap a passenger and they walk in the direction of their arrow. Clear the crowd by getting everyone off and onto the right train — in the right order.

HOW TO PLAY
• Orange passengers with bags are getting off — they step out of the door and head for the stairs.
• Everyone else is boarding. Their shirt colour tells you which train: green Slow, blue Fast, purple AC.
• Someone still in the doorway? Nobody gets on. Let them get off first!
• Tap in the wrong order and the crowd gets stuck. Undo and try again.

FEATURES
• 29 stations from Churchgate to Virar — then the next round with bigger crowds
• 1, 2 and then 3 trains at once on island platforms
• Pillars and chai stalls in the way
• Stars, coins and best times for every station
• Hints when you're stuck, free undo, no time pressure
• 3D tour that teaches you in a minute
• Plays offline, one finger, short sessions

Pehle utarne do, phir chadho! Download Next Station and become the station master.
